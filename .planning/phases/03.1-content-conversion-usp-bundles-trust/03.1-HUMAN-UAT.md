### GAP-02: CatalogBundles intro reads as three disconnected sub-sections

**status:** failed
**UAT step:** 4
**Severity:** blocks phase close (SC2)

**Observed:** The current composition stacks eyebrow + H2 + intro ("FLAVOR + PACK" → "Pick your CAPSHOT." → "Two steps. Pick a flavor you'll actually drink, then pick a pack that matches how often you'll use it.") above FlavorTeaser's own H3 ("Pick your flavor.") and later Bundles' H3 ("Pick your pack."). The three stacked bold headings read as three independent sections — the user has to actually read the two-step intro paragraph to understand the two sub-sections are steps of one flow.

**What's needed:**
Pick EITHER (a) OR (b):
- **(a)** Replace the current intro with explicit numbered-step scaffolding — "Step 1 / Pick your flavor" and "Step 2 / Pick your pack" rendered as prominent step markers (e.g., a small circled "1" or "01" before each sub-heading), so the visual connection to the parent H2 is immediate.
- **(b)** Rework the parent H2 to be more declarative and action-forward (not "Pick your CAPSHOT." which is passive and the exact same verb as the two sub-headings) — something like "Build your CAPSHOT in two steps." — AND tighten the intro to one short sentence so the sub-headings feel like continuations, not restarts.

Preference: (a) is clearer and leaves the brand voice untouched; (b) is lighter-weight but fragile. Planner should pick one with rationale.

**Files likely touched:** `src/sections/CatalogBundles.tsx`, `src/sections/FlavorTeaser.tsx` (CAREFUL — D3.1-05a byte-identical lock may need relaxing for sub-heading change; if (a) is picked and FlavorTeaser's own H2/H3 is suppressed inside CatalogBundles, D3.1-05a needs an update entry), `src/sections/Bundles.tsx`, `tests/e2e/catalog-bundles.spec.ts`.
