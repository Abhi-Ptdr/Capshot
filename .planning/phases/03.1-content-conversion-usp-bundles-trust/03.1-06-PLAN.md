<objective>
Close UAT GAP-02 (CatalogBundles intro reads as three disconnected sub-sections) by implementing approach (a) from the UAT doc: explicit numbered step scaffolding ("01 / Step 1 — Pick your flavor" and "02 / Step 2 — Pick your pack") that makes the visual connection to the parent H2 "Pick your CAPSHOT." immediate. Keep FlavorTeaser.tsx BYTE-IDENTICAL (D3.1-05a lock honored). Remove Bundles.tsx's current intro eyebrow+H3 band so the parent step marker is the sole "Step 2" scaffolding and the tri-card lane follows directly. Extend `tests/e2e/catalog-bundles.spec.ts` in-place.

Purpose: Restore ROADMAP SC2 (Phase 3.1): "Visitor on `/` sees a combined Product Catalog & Pricing Bundles section" — the real-device UAT walkthrough on 2026-10-11 found the current composition reads as three stacked bold headings (parent H2, FlavorTeaser's H3, Bundles' H3) instead of one two-step flow. The user has to read the two-step intro paragraph to understand the two sub-sections are steps of one flow. Explicit numbered step markers eliminate this cognitive load.

Output: Modified `src/sections/CatalogBundles.tsx` (adds two step markers as explicit visual scaffolding); modified `src/sections/Bundles.tsx` (removes the inline eyebrow+H3 since the parent now owns Step 2 scaffolding); modified `tests/e2e/catalog-bundles.spec.ts` (adds CB7/CB8/CB9 for step markers, preserves CB1-CB6/CB-RM-1).

**Approach rationale (why (a) not (b)):** The UAT doc explicitly prefers (a): "(a) is clearer and leaves the brand voice untouched; (b) is lighter-weight but fragile." Approach (b) (rewording parent H2 + tightening intro) would require re-reviewing Hinglish copy and depends on wording nuance to carry the two-step signal; approach (a) is deterministic scaffolding that works regardless of copy weight. Approach (a) also future-proofs the pattern — if Phase 4 reorders CatalogBundles children, the step markers travel with the parent.

**Why FlavorTeaser stays byte-identical:** D3.1-05a locks FlavorTeaser.tsx as byte-identical in the Phase 3.1 relocation. GAP-02 "What's needed" note explicitly says "CAREFUL — D3.1-05a byte-identical lock may need relaxing for sub-heading change". We AVOID relaxing it by placing the Step 1 marker in the parent wrapper (CatalogBundles.tsx) as a sibling ABOVE FlavorTeaser, not inside FlavorTeaser. FlavorTeaser's own H3 "Pick your flavor." stays — it functions as the content heading under the "Step 1" visual scaffolding. (If visual review later feels the duplicate reads awkwardly, follow-up can introduce a FlavorTeaser prop to toggle its H3 — out of scope for GAP-02 closure.)

**Why Bundles.tsx intro is restructured:** Bundles.tsx (Phase 3.1 Plan 03, NOT byte-identical-locked) currently renders its own eyebrow "Step 2 — Pack chuno." + H3 "Pick your pack." inside the tri-card lane. With the parent CatalogBundles now owning the "02 / Step 2" scaffolding, keeping both would re-create the "three stacked bold headings" problem the UAT flagged. Clean fix: delete the Bundles.tsx intro eyebrow+H3+paragraph band; the parent step marker becomes the single "Step 2" scaffolding above the tri-card `<ul>`. The tri-card `<ul>` + per-card content + CTAs stay byte-equivalent.
</objective>
